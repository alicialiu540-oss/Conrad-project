SIMPLE STEPS. MEANINGFUL DETAIL.

From recording to understanding.

01 /

Record naturally

Place your camera somewhere steady, use good lighting, and keep the joints you want to analyze in view.

02 /

Let motion become data

Our program detects body landmarks and calculates joint angles frame by frame. The LSTM option learns from your uploaded reference and compares the two movement sequences.

03 /

Explore your analysis

Watch the annotated video, inspect angle timelines, and download your measurements for further research.

i

Built for exploration, with care. Conrad is a research prototype. Video-based angles are 2D estimates affected by camera position and visibility. Results are not a diagnosis or a substitute for a qualified clinician’s assessment.
